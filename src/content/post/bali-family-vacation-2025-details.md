## Learnings ✍🏼

1. **Use a zero forex credit card wherever possible.**  
   The surcharge you pay on certain transactions is still cheaper than the money you lose by converting INR → USD → IDR.

2. **Always pay the Visa fees with a zero forex credit card.**  
   Never pay in cash like we did—seriously, the exchange losses are brutal.

3. **Carry a forex card that allows withdrawals.**  
   This saves you from the hassle (and double conversion loss) of carrying INR → USD → IDR. We didn’t have one this time, but definitely a must for next time!

4. **Bargain a lot.**  
   You’ll be surprised at how much cheaper things get if you haggle a little at the markets.

5. **Be extra careful at money exchanges.**  
   Especially at places where the exchange rate looks “too good to be true” or the exchanges that don’t ask for your passport - count the notes in front of them!

6. **There’s a lot of walking to do.**  
   Wear good shoes, carry water and some energising snacks—you’ll thank yourself later.

7. **Watch out for monkeys.**  
   Especially at Uluwatu or any monkey forest. They can (and will) snatch your stuff—we learned this the hard way.

8. **Food tip for non-vegetarians.**  
   Ask your driver to take you to a local warung (where locals eat). It’s much cheaper and more authentic—but beware of the infamous “Bali belly.” We were vegetarians, so options were limited.
